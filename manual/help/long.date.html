Format a month and day as a calendar label

Description

Format a month and day for reporting recurring solar or stellar events within a year.

Usage

long.date(date)

Arguments

date

date in 'MM-DD' format

Details

A season or event table may need a day/month label without an epoch. The first two characters are the month number and characters four and five the day number; the separator occupies position three. The month is looked up in R's English month abbreviations. This formatting step does not validate a calendar date or convert between calendars.

Value

Character month-and-day labels such as "21 Jun", derived from the supplied month and day.

See Also

time2jd, jd2time, timestring, BC.AD

Examples

long.date('01-01')
long.date('08-23')
