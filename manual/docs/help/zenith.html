Function index · zenith() method

Estimate solar declination at the zenith

Description

Determine the solar declination associated with an overhead passage at the observer's latitude, where such a passage is possible.

Usage

zenith(loc, parallax = 0.00224, altitude = 90, year = skyscapeR.env$cur.year)

Arguments

loc

This can be either the latitude of the location, or a skyscapeR.horizon object.

parallax

Mean horizontal parallax in degrees. The default is 0.00224 degrees for the Sun.

altitude

Altitude used in the approximate parallax correction. Defaults to 90 degrees.

year

Astronomical year used to determine the tropical latitude limit.

Details

The geometric zenith has declination equal to the site latitude. The function checks whether the latitude lies within the obliquity of the requested epoch. At the geometric zenith the altitude is 90 degrees and the parallax correction vanishes. An explicitly supplied altitude invokes the approximate correction at that altitude. The function returns NULL outside the permitted band. This supplies a declination criterion rather than the dates of the passages.

Value

Solar declination in degrees with the requested parallax correction, or NULL when the latitude falls outside the solstitial limits used by the function.

Validation

A05 verified the geometric zenith declination and tropical-boundary eligibility at two epochs. See the A05 check in the technical manual.

See Also

jS, dS, eq, antizenith, spatial.equinox, parallax.corr

Examples

# Zenith sun declination for Mexico City:
zenith(19.419)

# There is no zenith sun phenomenon in London:
zenith(51.507)
