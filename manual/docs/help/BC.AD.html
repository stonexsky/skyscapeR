Convert an astronomical year to a BC or AD label

Description

Label an astronomical year in historical BC or AD notation when reporting an epoch.

Usage

BC.AD(year)

Arguments

year

Numeric astronomical year or vector of years. Year zero is 1 BC. Historical labels such as "3700 BC" are accepted by astroYear.

Details

Astronomical dating includes a year zero, whereas historical BC and AD labels run directly from 1 BC to AD 1. Positive years retain their number and receive AD. For zero or a negative year, subtract the astronomical year from one and append BC. The conversion works element by element and changes only the label, not the calendar or instant.

Value

Character labels for astronomical years. Year 0 is 1 BC and year -1 is 2 BC.

See Also

time2jd, jd2time, timestring, long.date

Examples

BC.AD(100)
BC.AD(0)
BC.AD(-1)
BC.AD(-99)
BC.AD(-100)
