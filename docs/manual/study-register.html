Sky rendering

A full grid sweep recovered all 4,323,600 stored Milky Way classes. The Carina calibration field recovered 20.700 V mag arcsec⁻². The publication-colour and publication-mono checks exercised the default, explicit switches, zero extinction and a one-minute time step. For an extinction coefficient of 0.2 mag per airmass the transmission was 0.149 at 5° and 0.832 at 90°. The natural-renderer flux suite recovered the Sirius flux at three field widths and checked the Pleiades, Moon and five visible planets. The mean absolute residual against 22 held-out twilight measurements from Koomen et al. was 0.481 mag. Reproduction files.

The heliacal-rising study gives the full comparison between the phase solver and the radiance threshold. The S02 study gives the false-positive rates for azimuth-defined nulls tested in declination.

Results and priorities

The five package validation studies report completed tests of near-pole transformations, off-Carina photometry, withheld-station horizon errors, partly occluded features and stellar astrometry. D02 evaluates direct propagation near the celestial pole. The next empirical priority is fitting and testing diffuse brightness on independent, consistently masked V-band fields. For terrain uncertainty, the withheld-station coverage provides a baseline for models using station and survey characteristics.
