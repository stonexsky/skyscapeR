Convert a historical year to astronomical numbering

Description

Specify the epoch of an observation with historical era labels.

Usage

astroYear(x)

Arguments

x

Numeric astronomical years, character integer years, or historical labels.

Details

Astronomical years include zero. Historical BC or BCE years convert by subtracting the historical year from one. AD and CE years keep their value. Era labels are case-insensitive and follow a positive whole year.

Value

Numeric astronomical years in input order.

See Also

BC.AD, timestring

Examples

astroYear(c("3700 BC", "1 BCE", "1 AD", "2026 CE"))
